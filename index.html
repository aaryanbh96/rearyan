About Aryan

I’m not a software developer by training — I’m an operator who sees businesses as systems. At The Tao of Tea that means working every layer at once: running revenue channels (Faire from zero to Top Shop status, eight consecutive months, zero late shipments; price increases secured on Amazon and iHerb), building the data and software layer (forecasting, demand planning, and the production systems that automate our warehouse), and partnering with the founder on strategy — expansion, liquidation, compliance, whatever the quarter demands. When a QuickBooks migration had been stuck for eleven years, I did it in a week without disrupting operations.

My approach: see the whole system, find the broken loop, build the fix, get it adopted. MBA for the business judgment, MS in Data Science for the analytical depth, AI-assisted development for the build speed — and a system dynamics habit of looking for feedback loops everywhere. Before this: Chief of Staff at an early-stage startup, and five years of human-judgment work evaluating search quality for ML systems.

I’m fascinated by history, civilizations, and how decisions shape events. Outside work: reading, chess, and rich storytelling — shout out to Red Dead Redemption 2 and Halo. One day, I hope to build something of my own.
