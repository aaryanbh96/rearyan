About Aryan

I’m a strategic operator with a unique combination of business acumen from my MBA and analytical depth from my MS in Data Science. My background spans Chief of Staff role, founder-facing work, and consulting—always focused on turning ideas into results and getting things done. I work best where strategy, data, and execution meet.

Currently, I work as a Data and Growth Consultant where I streamline business processes, lead data initiatives, and execute special projects that drive growth. My approach blends first principles thinking, systems thinking, and a hands-on operating style. My priorities right now are to have a steep learning curve, work across functions, own initiatives, and contribute to strategy—ideally as close to the heart of the company as possible, alongside founders.

I’m fascinated by history, civilizations, and how decisions shape the course of events. Outside work, I enjoy reading, gaming, and immersing myself in rich storytelling—shout out to Red Dead Redemption 2 and Halo. One day, I hope to be a founder or a member of a founding team.
